Game clock

Timing settings

Running = snap to snap, including the play itself. Our tempo default is 25. Stopped = just the play, about 6. The clock stops on incompletions, scores, turnovers, penalties and tries, and on first downs inside the last 2:00 of each half. Out of bounds isn't charted, so nudge the clock when it happens.

Yardage follows the NCAA rulebook used by the UIL. Enforced from the previous spot, with half the distance to the goal when needed. Flag after the play (late hit)? Log the play and spot the ball first, then add the penalty: it enforces from that spot.
