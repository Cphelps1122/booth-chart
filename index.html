How it works

From Hudl to the headset in three steps.

Step 1 · Prep

Upload the scout

Export the opponent from Hudl and drop it in. Solutions builds the report and loads their tendencies into the game-day chart.

Step 2 · Game day

Chart it live

One coach per side taps in each snap from the press box. Every staff device sees it instantly, with no tablet hand-offs.

Step 3 · Between series

Get the answer

The OC and DC reports update after every play: what they're doing, what's working, and what's coming next.

Inside the chart

Made by a staff that calls plays at tempo.

Tap-the-field spotting

No yard-line math. Tap or slide the ball to the spot and the gain, down and distance update. Nudge by a yard if the officials move it.

Situational tendencies

Shell, box and pressure by down and distance, field zone, hash and formation, with anything over 70% highlighted so it jumps off the screen.

Penalties enforced for you

Pick the penalty and Solutions applies the yardage, half-the-distance and automatic first downs to the down and distance.

Secure game codes

Every game has its own code, so a wrong link or a wrong tap never switches the press box to a different game.

Exports

Every report goes out as a PDF or Excel file after the game, ready for the film room.
