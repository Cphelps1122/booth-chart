Questions

How do we pay?

Most schools pay by purchase order or check. Send the form below and we'll email an invoice. Card payment is coming soon.

Can we try it first?

Yes. Pick "Free trial" below and we'll set your staff up to chart a scrimmage or a game for free.

What if our plan runs out?

Nothing is deleted. Your games, reports and stats stay viewable; you just can't chart new games until you renew.

Do we still need Hudl?

Yes. Solutions reads the Hudl exports you already make. It adds live calls, scouting and predictions on top.
